!Limitations: highlight & pill presets

Glass Pill, Highlight Select, Pill Soft / Solid / Outline, Soft Marker and Underline Draw animate their letters with fixed keyframes (no lag). Keyframes do not move by themselves, so:

  • Trimming the start of a clip (or pasting a trimmed copy) leaves its letters out of step with the highlight box.
  • A clip changed while the panel was closed stays out of step until the panel is opened again.
  • Clips placed before this update keep their old animation until placed again.

Timing and every preset add-on (box size / position, highlight colour, row gap / position, pill text colour, Kinetic settings) are edited in this panel: the timing bar and Clip controls on the Use tab show the selected clip's own controls. They are hidden in Resolve's Inspector, so with the panel closed they cannot be changed (clips placed before this update still show them there).

How to avoid it: keep this panel open while you edit (minimised is fine). It fixes these clips by itself: every few seconds it checks the timeline's highlight / pill clips and re-keys any that were trimmed or retimed, and when it opens it checks them all once. Before a render, open the panel and give it a few seconds.
